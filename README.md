[ALPHA-BUILD, USABLE] 

This is an application that measures and tracks the user's typing speed. 
Current functionality includes practice typing, timed typing, exporting data from timed attempts 
as .csv and .txt, and changing the theme and music. 

Practice Mode includes toggling between full sentences and random words, with ranges of 1 to 5 
sentences and 10 to 50 random words. An attempt starts after the first valid keystroke. 
After an attempt is complete, your accuracy, words per minute, and overall typing score 
(from 0 to 10) will display. You may click the "Reset" button during or after an attempt to 
practice again. If you change the number of sentences or random words during an attempt, the
attempt will be reset. Practice attempt data are not recorded. From the Practice Mode page, 
you may go directly to Timed, Home, or Statistics [IN-PROGRESS].

Timed Mode includes the same functionality as Practice Mode; however, after an attempt is complete, 
your attempt time, accuracy, words per minute, and overall typing score (from 0 to 10) will display. 
The app records timed attempt data to a local database in the application's directory. From the 
Timed Mode page, you may go directly to Practice, Home, or Statistics [IN-PROGRESS]. 

You may export your data from timed attempts as a .csv or .txt file. The .csv option is for you to 
do as you wish with your data seamlessly, and the .txt option is for a quick, formatted view of your 
data. Each file type has optional data you may include in your export. [IN-PROGRESS: .docx, .pdf, .xlsx]

There are six genres of music to pick from: Classical, Dubstep, Jazz, Pop, Rock, and Techno. You 
may change to another genre at any point. There is also a control panel at the top of the page 
where you can go to the previous track, next track, or pause/play the current track. The end of 
a track should [IN-PROGRESS] start the next track, and the genre's track list is circular.

There are four themes you can switch between at any point: Original, Nature, Purple Paradise, 
and Tron. The Tron theme contains 11 variations based on the secondary color; black is always 
the primary color.

All music was sourced from Pixabay, an open-source media platform where artists post their work. 
The Credits page includes attributions for all these artists based on their available profile 
details on Pixabay.
